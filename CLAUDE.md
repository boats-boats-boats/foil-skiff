# CLAUDE.md — Project Context & Engineering Guidelines for `foil-skiff`

## 1. Project Overview
`foil-skiff` (**FoilSkiff**) is an R&D, hydrodynamics, and fabrication repository whose **PRIMARY ARCHITECTURE** is the **Central-Keel 4-Float Wide-Staggered U-Foil with Dry Above-Water 4-Channel Wing-Warping & Twin Fixed Differential Pump-Jets (`Models 13 & 14`, see [`WING_WARP_UFOIL_JET.md`](WING_WARP_UFOIL_JET.md))**—with the **Propeller Track** (Split STOL Flaperon & Flush-Nested Switchblade pods) preserved as secondary high-speed/narrow-beam options.

* **Primary Reference Hull:** **`True Kit Navigator 300`** (`300 × 155 cm` [some revisions `170 cm`], `60–70 cm` inside beam, `Ø460 mm` continuous closed curved bow tubes, factory notched `Tru-Alu` rigid aluminum transom rated to `10 HP` short/extra-short shaft, `31 kg` bare weight, `500 kg` capacity, `105 × 60 × 30 cm` bag). **IMPORTANT TUNNEL REALITY CHECK:** True Kit does NOT publish exact tunnel height; real-world `Navigator 300` welded PVC tunnel clearance is approximately **`~75–100 mm` (`3"–4"`)** above flat ground (NOT `230 mm`!). Always tape-measure actual `H_tunnel_mid` and `H_tunnel_transom` before cutting struts, and use stern launching wheels or outer-tube clearance for rocky beaching.
* **Secondary Open-Bow Reference:** **`True Kit Discovery 330`** (`330 × 165 cm`, `36 kg`) and **`Takacat LX`** (open square bow enabling a full rigid Bow-to-Transom O-Frame).

Primary reference documents: [`WING_WARP_UFOIL_JET.md`](WING_WARP_UFOIL_JET.md) **(PRIMARY)**, [`electric-foiling-dinghy-guide.md`](electric-foiling-dinghy-guide.md), [`QUAD_SWATH_TANDEM_JET.md`](QUAD_SWATH_TANDEM_JET.md), [`SPLIT_KEEL_JET_CCV_POD.md`](SPLIT_KEEL_JET_CCV_POD.md), [`BREAKAWAY_WING_SOCKET.md`](BREAKAWAY_WING_SOCKET.md), [`DESIGN_TRADE_OFFS.md`](DESIGN_TRADE_OFFS.md), and [`VISUAL_ATLAS.md`](VISUAL_ATLAS.md).

## 1B. PRIMARY Architecture Rules (Central-Keel 4-Float Wing-Warp Jet U-Foil — Do Not Violate!)
1. **Single `28 L` Central Keel Pod (`NO` Outer Submerged Torpedoes):** Centralizes `5.38 kWh` `IP68` batteries + `2 × Flipsky 75100` `VESC`s + **twin fixed `Ø108 mm` wide-bore rear differential pump-jets** (`2 × Ø54 mm` slim inrunners, `132.5 cm²` total exit, `0` moving tail parts, `~60%` wire-to-thrust efficiency).
2. **Two Wide-Staggered `1.80 m` U-Foils (`65/35` Lift Split, `+130 mm` Vertical Stagger):** Front Main U-Foil (`900 cm²`, `320 mm` deep in cruise) + Rear Staggered U-Foil (`500 cm²`, `190 mm` deep, clean above front wake, replaces separate tail stabilizer). Always include `4×` horizontal anti-ventilation fences on the `18 mm` lower vertical blades (`Z = -570 mm`).
3. **Four Stepped Corner Tip Floats (`4 × 24 L = 96 L`) — 3-Stage Flight on `600 mm` Struts:** Top view = `NACA 0014` torpedo (`620 × 135 mm`), Side view = rounded-corner vertical rectangle (`620 × 400 mm`, `R = 65 mm`). `0–5 kn` Takeoff (`75%` submerged, `~100 kgf` pre-lift) $\rightarrow$ `6–10 kn` Low Foil (`150 mm` wet, linear 4-corner stability) $\rightarrow$ `12–15.5 kn` Cruise (`+30 mm` DRY IN AIR, `0 N` float water drag $\rightarrow$ `~33 nm` @ `12 kn` / `~20.5 nm` @ `15.5 kn`).
4. **Parallel-Float Dry 4-Channel Wing-Warping & Mandatory Divergence Safety (`>50–60 kn`):**
   * Front & Rear floats stay **`100%` rigid & parallel**, permanently joined by **solid fixed-length `Ø34 mm` above-water carbon tie-bars (`ZERO` telescoping slip-tubes!)**. Each `18 mm` blade pivots on a **fixed `Ø10 mm` `316L` lower pin** at the float bottom and extends `320 mm` up inside a free-flooding center trunk (fore/aft foam chambers `100%` sealed & unsinkable), where a dry `$35` `IP67` `Tr8` linear actuator sweeps the upper tang **`±17 mm`** (`~100 N`, `0 W` hold) for **`±3.0°` foil AoA twist** (`0.06%` strain).
   * **Mandatory:** Place the `0°` UD carbon bending spar dead-on **`25%–28%` chord** ($e \le 3\text{ mm}$) + `1` ply `±45°` torsion skin + strictly non-backdrivable `Tr8` linear actuators locking the tang so static torsional divergence stays above `50–60+ kn`.

## 2. Core Engineering Rules & Physical Constraints (Do Not Violate)

When generating hydrodynamic calculations, CAD/molds, 3D-print fairings, battery layouts, controller wiring, or BOMs in this repository, always adhere to these physical and electrical constraints:

1. **Primary Architecture — `True Kit Navigator 300` Bow-Contoured Hard Deck + Deep-Submerged Split STOL Flaperon Flying-Keel Pod:**
   * **Bow-Contoured Hard Deck & Bow-Bent Under-Hull Plate (NEVER Flat Rectangles! NEVER Boxes Above Top Deck!):**
     * Both the top `28 mm` 2-piece Hard Deck (`~5.5–7.5 kg` carbon / `~9–10 kg` Alu honeycomb) AND the under-hull carriage plate **must follow the real hull contour—tapering in planform to the curved bow tube and bending smoothly upward (`~85–95 mm` bow rocker) in the bow** so zero sharp rectangular corners chafe the PVC or catch waves.
     * **ZERO protruding servo boxes, enclosures, or hardware above the top Hard Deck:** The cockpit floor must remain `100%` flush for passengers and gear. Route **twin marine-anodized L-Track rails (`390 mm` spacing, `1,570 mm` long) `100%` flush inside the top carbon skin** so **1 or 2 `424 mm`-wide ergonomic low-sit chairs** (`125 mm` pan height, `22°` recline, right-hand armrest wireless helm pod) slide/lock in `5 seconds` (Solo centered @ CoG, Tandem inline inside the `600 mm` tunnel, or `0`-chair flush cargo deck).
   * **Zero-Hole Top-to-Bottom Coupling Across the Continuous `1.2 mm` PVC Tunnel Web:**
     * **`Navigator 300` Primary (Option B1) — Bow-Contoured `Tru-Alu` Transom-Clamped Hybrid C-Frame + `6 mm` Dyneema Bow-D-Ring Sling (`~3.0–3.8 kg`):** Clamp twin `50 × 20 × 2.5 mm` `6061-T6` Alu rails (spaced **`250 mm` apart** → `~165 mm` clear per side inside the `600 mm` tunnel; `220 mm` pod retracts *between* rails) directly onto the factory `10 HP`-rated `Tru-Alu` transom (zero holes) and tie the upturned bow ski-nose forward via a **`6 mm` Dyneema sling** to the factory **Bow Towing D-Ring** (`3×–4×` lower bending, Delrin shear pin). Place the `4×` parallelogram pivots at **`x_front = -540 mm`, `x_rear = +220 mm`** so the `86.5°` folded `1.3 m` pod clears beneath the `Tru-Alu` transom within the `300 cm` LOA (`~130–140 mm` / `5.1–5.5"` sand clearance; verify exact `10–30 mm` `Tru-Alu` lower-lip dip on hull).
     * **`Navigator 300` Universal Alternative (Option A) — Flush-Recessed Switchable Magnetic Clamp (`4 ×` `10 × 10 cm` `Magswitch` rotary `N52` / EPMs, `~800 kgf` across `~2.5 mm` gap):** Recessed flush inside the `28 mm` Hard Deck directly over the `1.2 mm` PVC web (never through `100 mm` air deck!), micro-ribbed rubber grip pads (`μ ≈ 0.65–0.80`), aft `Tru-Alu` transom shear/safety bridle, never plain electromagnets, hermetic saltwater sealing, and 6-DOF (gyro+accel only) IMU mode.
     * **Secondary Open-Bow Reference (Option B2) — Bow-Contoured Rigid "O-Frame" Clamshell (`~2.5–3.5 kg`):** For open-bow `True Kit Discovery 330` / `Takacat LX`.
     * **Factory OEM Welded Centerboard Slot (Option C, `~1.0–1.5 kg`, OEM Only):** Single RF-welded `60 × 320 mm` slot + PVC boot + split-neoprene gasket in non-airtight tunnel web.
   * **Mandatory 4-Bar Parallelogram Strut Geometry (`4×` Ultra-Slim Side-Straddling Blades or `2×` Recessed Center Struts):**
     * **Primary A (`4×` Side-Straddling Tandem Blades — `60 × 9 mm` solid UD carbon or `75 × 10 mm` `6061-T6` Alu, 2 left + 2 right):** Bolts straight from both `250 mm`-spaced rails to pod sides (`0` mid-strut crossbars, `0` pod slot cut-out; rigid closed rectangle formed by top rails + rigid `220 mm` pod, `9.8×–10.5×` buckling safety), folds to **`~90 mm` below PVC web (`~135–140 mm` / `5.5"` clear above beach!)**, `11.4 lbf` drag @ `15.5 kn` (`0.5 lbf` spray drag; route thin charge/signal leads in a `3 mm` trailing-edge channel). **True `90°` Upper (`13 mm`) & Lower (`12 mm`) Fold Channels** fold Main Wing (`2 × 102 = 204 mm`) and secondary wings flush inside the **`220 mm` pod width** (fits cleanly **between the `250 mm` under-hull rails**, `100%` intact `26 L` dry bay, driven by closed-loop `Ø2.5 mm` `SK78` Dyneema coaxial pulleys, zero underwater sweep motors!).
     * **Primary B (`2×` Center Tandem Hollow Carbon Struts — `100 × 18 mm`):** `9.1 lbf` drag, requires offset pins (`+24 mm` TE top, `-24 mm` LE bottom) + `55 mm` pod top recess (`~135 mm` folded depth). **Mandatory Asymmetric-Load, True `90°` Fold Channels & Dual Wet `IP68` Servo-Pin Hardware (See `BREAKAWAY_WING_SOCKET.md`):** (1) **True `90°` Upper (`13 mm` Roof) & Lower (`12 mm` Belly) Fold Channels (`220 mm` Total Dart — `100%` Intact `26 L` Dry Bay!):** Main Wing halves pivot on `Ø16 mm` vertical pins at `y = ±54 mm` inside a `12 mm` belly channel (`40 mm` inboard UHMW overhang roots carry `100%` lift/roll at `~35 MPa`, `>10×` safety), synchronized by **intermeshing `90°` `316` SS gear sectors at `y = 0`**, deployed/folded by **closed-loop `Ø2.5 mm` `SK78` Dyneema + coaxial strut-hinge pulleys (`<8 kgf`, `0` sweep motors)**, and locked by a **Wet `IP68` `75 kg·cm` Servo Variable-Preload Roller Pin** (`110 mm` slider lug → `20°` hardened `316` SS roller `μ ≈ 0.08` → `3.5:1` rocker → grease-booted `37 kgf` `316` SS spring → `12 mm` over-center cam: `0%` Fold/Deploy ↔ `50%` Reef `~45 kgf` ↔ `100%` Cruise TDC `~85 kgf` tip / `~170 kgf` center at **`0 W` holding power**, `5-sec` pull-to-reset, zero broken pins, **`179 mm` clear ahead of tail prop**!). Active flaperons embed a **rotating wet `IP68` foil-base servo** (20 mm flat direct-drive or 15 mm leveraged horn inside the folding root carrier + soft 3-wire flex pigtail through a stationary static gland — commands exact `0.0°` flat-lock before folding, never relies on water flow, zero dynamic O-rings; `+80 mm` across intact dry bay for Switchblade/Staggerwing, or clears below belly during `~5 s` DOWN STOL). Keep **External `y = ±125 mm` Foil-Base Knuckles** (`~350 mm` dart) as bolt-on alt for unmodified `80 mm` pods and **Fixed `4× M8` Bolted** as zero-fold fallback; reject sideways `102 mm` pod-core cutouts (`93%` bay loss), solenoids, detaching belly-leash V-cradles & single-tip breakaway hinges; (2) **Upper End:** `260 mm` tapered T-Head yoke on a full-width `Ø16 mm` stainless axle spanning BOTH `250 mm` rails (`8.3×` lower pin load) + anti-splay bridge plate + flanged UHMW/bronze thrust washers (`μ ≈ 0.08`) + identical **Wet `IP68` `75 kg·cm` Servo Variable-Preload Roller Lock** on the `100 mm` front strut LE tang (`0%` 1-Button `BEACH MODE` ↔ `50%` Shoal `~70 kgf` ↔ `100%` Cruise TDC `~140 kgf` at `0 W`, `0 kgf` pin load in cruise since thrust presses LE onto solid Alu stop, auto-resets when lowered, Smart Divider trips struts before `~170 kgf` wings; manual `M6` SS emergency pin hole on both locks); (3) **Lower End:** `180 mm` flared pod saddle T-foot on a `Ø16 mm` axle anchored into twin `6 mm` `G10`/carbon full-height internal pod bulkheads + `70 mm` forward compression stop + `±45°` carbon torsion wrap + stainless crab-pot line cutter.
     * **NEVER pivot a single central "inverted-T" mast on a `1.3 m` pod** (tail smashes ceiling @ `40°`, nose plows `27 cm` deeper to `-91 cm`) and **NEVER use thick `4×` quad struts (`110 × 15 mm`, `18.7 lbf` drag)**.
   * **Submerged Lifting Fuselage Bay (`~1.3 m × 220 mm × 80 mm`, `26 L` Buoyancy, `400 mm` Deep) + Swappable `IP68` Battery Cartridges + Internal Flaperon Servos:**
     * Orient batteries **longitudinally** as **two removable, factory-potted `IP68` `2.5–2.7 kWh` (`~14 kg` ea.) slide-in cartridges** (`~26.6 kg` free Archimedes buoyancy; 1-cartridge + foam dummy plug option for solo hops).
     * House **both `IP67` brushless `70–80 kg·cm` flaperon servos (`40 × 20 × 40 mm` ea., `0.19 L` combined)**, **`ESP32` + 6-DOF `BNO085` IMU**, seawater-cooled **`Flipsky 75100` `VESC`s**, and tailcone **`Flipsky 65161` pods** inside the same waterproof fuselage bay so **ZERO high-current DC cables, ZERO deck servo boxes, and ZERO external `650 mm` strut pushrods** are needed!
   * **Approved Deep-Submerged High-Lift Wing Architectures (100% `400 mm` Deep — Zero Surface-Piercing Wave Slam!):**
     * **PRIMARY — Submerged Split STOL Flaperons (`1,000 cm²` Main + `2 × 250 cm²` Active Split Slotted Flaperons, Internal Pod Servos):** `7.2 kn` takeoff, `29.8 nm` @ `15.5 kn`. Mount slightly below/behind (*Junkers* STOL slot, `CL_max ≈ 1.95–2.10`) or permanently above/behind (*Fixed Upper Staggerwing*, `Z = +35 mm`, `+0.5 kn` takeoff, zero sled, always out of wake & shielded) — **OR combine both via the `100%` Internal-Pod Morphing STOL-to-Staggerwing Elevator Sled Upgrade** (`90 mm` pod encloses the entire mechanism with **ZERO external swing-arms, belts/gears, or sliding dry-hull seals**: Main Wing at `Z = -22 mm` + free-flooding internal wing-box bounded by twin curved watertight bulkheads + twin `Ø10 mm` guide rods + self-locking `Tr10×2` leadscrew (`0 W` hold) shifting `70 mm` vertically between `Z = -35 mm` DOWN optimal `6–8 mm` Venturi STOL slot and `Z = +35 mm` UP Protected Staggerwing `57 mm` / `4–5×` above wake; `2×` `IP68` billet pitch servos ride directly ON the sled through flush silicone cheek wiper skirts, with only a static bulkhead wire gland entering the dry bay!). Reject external protruding `58 mm` cheek swing-arms (hidden belt/gear transfer & kelp snags). Direct-drive from internal pod servos via **`Ø8 mm` 316L stainless torque tubes + CNC double-O-ring / Viton lip-seal glands** at **`~20%` chord** (`≤3.6 N·m` / `37 kg·cm` peak torque, `>40×` shaft safety, **hydrodynamically self-centering to `0°`** on power loss). Delivers 4-in-1 takeoff flaps, Direct-Lift wave/elevation leveling, active roll leveling & banked turning (`~$160` active BOM).
     * **SECONDARY — Flush-Nested Top-Deck Switchblade (`+1,300 cm²` Roof-Nested + `1,250 cm²` Lower Thin Wing):** Two `680 × 105 × 13 mm` solid UD carbon blades (`AR ≈ 15` open) recessed `100%` flush into a `13 mm` roof pocket (`+10 mm` pod height `80 → 90 mm`, `0 cm²` extra wet area & `0` exposed tips when closed >`9 kn`, `6.7 kn` takeoff, `30.8 nm` @ `15.5 kn`, **`32.3 lbf` best pod drag @ `22 kn`**). Mandatory: `70 mm` carbon overhang bridge reacting lift in compression, `4×` blades at `x = 170 / 960 mm` clearing the `680 mm` sweep arc, `2–3 mm` aft sand flush slots + UHMW wear pads, hydrodynamic aft self-closing latch, and internal pod aft auto-trim.
   * **EXPLICITLY REJECTED Wing Concepts (See `DESIGN_TRADE_OFFS.md` — Do NOT Re-Introduce!):**
     * **NEVER use Upper Surface-Piercing "Step-Out" Wings (Forward OR Deep Aft) for open-water chop:** `850×` air-to-water shock at `15.5 kn` (`q = 32.8 kPa`) causes `-140 kgf` nose-dive suction + `103 lbf` brake or `+210 kgf` (`+1G`) bow launch (forward), and `+150 kgf` tail-up slam (`-1,350 N·m` bow-down) + `~45 cm` CoL jump (deep aft).
     * **NEVER use external un-nested submerged swing-wings** (`+33%–53%` cruise drag), **split battery-fuselage swing-wings** (`100 A` cable pivot fatigue), or **fixed thick full-span battery wings** (`3,400+ cm²`, `t/c ≥ 14%`, `2.2×–2.7×` drag).
   * **Mandatory Submerged-Pod Safety Package:** `20 mm` sacrificial Kevlar/elastomer crush-nose bumper ahead of `5 mm` `G10` bulkhead, CNC double-O-ring rear bay door for swappable `IP68` cartridges, internal optical/conductivity leak sensor + cockpit alarm, `0.5 bar` pressure-relief burst valve, CNC double-O-ring/Viton rotary glands on `Ø8 mm` flaperon shafts, dry deck charging umbilical up the `3 mm` blade trailing channel, and `3:1` Dyneema tackle / gas-strut assisted retraction.

2. **Compact `4–13 kg` Low-`Kv` Drives & `100 Kv` vs. `120 Kv` Selection (`Flipsky 65161`):**
   * **Primary Pick:** Twin `IP68` sealed pods (**`2 × Flipsky 65161`** or **`2 × Maytech MTO65162`**, `4–6 kg` total, `6–10 kW` cont., `200–240 lbf`) + **`2 × Flipsky 75100` `VESC`** for zero-hardware `360°` CAN-bus differential power steering.
   * **Winding Selection Rule:** On **`16S LiFePO4` (`49.5 V` loaded)** pick **`100 Kv`** (`~4,160 RPM`, `+20%` torque/phase amp, `16–19.5 kn`, `21+ kn` w/ field weakening); on **`14S LiFePO4` (`<50 V`)** pick **`120 Kv`** (`~4,385 RPM`, `17–20 kn`). **Never use `195 Kv`** for direct-drive propellers (jet pumps only) or `350–500 Kv` RC motors without a planetary gearbox.
   * **Zero-Fab Turnkey Alt:** `2 × W-30` (`730 mm` tall shafts, `13.2 kg`, `6.4 kW` pk, `±85°` electric steer).
   * **Ultra-Budget Open-Can Alt (`2 × T63100`, `120–140 Kv`):** Epoxy-potted stator + ceramic bearings + fresh-water flush after every saltwater run.

3. **`14S` vs. `16S LiFePO4` ESC Voltage-Ceiling Rule:**
   * Stock `12S LiPo` ESCs (`APISQUEEN U30`, some `W-30`) max out at **`50.4 V`**, whereas `16S LiFePO4` charges to **`55.2–58.4 V`**. Use **`14S LiFePO4` (`<50 V`)** with stock `12S` ESCs, OR **`16S LiFePO4`** strictly with **`75 V`-rated `VESC`s (`Flipsky 75100` / `75200`)**.

4. **Round Outboard Shaft Tube Fairing Requirement (`NACA 0024`):**
   * A bare round shaft tube (`Ø36 mm × 0.50 m`, $C_D \approx 1.15$) wastes **`63 lbf` / `2.4 kW` at `10 knots`**. Any round-tube test mule (`Hangkai M2200`) above `6 knots` **must** use a `150 mm` chord **`NACA 0024` fairing** (`2,135 W` saved at `10 kn`).

5. **Dry Alternative & Pump-Jet Constraints:**
   * For rocky-beach simplicity or `16–22 kn` max speed, use the **dry `60 mm` 2-piece carbon/E-glass "Battery-Floor"** (`16S EVE LF105` `36.7 mm` flat cells) preserving the catamaran tunnel.
   * **Pump-Jet (`APISQUEEN BP100`, `10 kW`, `72–86 V`, `158 lbf`):** Cannot be paired with hydrofoils (flush intake sucks air on lift-off); strictly for prop-free `3"`-draft shallow beaching.
